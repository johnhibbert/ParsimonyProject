Keyword Finder

Modern job searching has changed. Your resume must somehow navigate through multiple layers of elaborate technological gatekeeping that prevent it from being seen by a human. Some professionals say that you MUST engage in 'keyword stuffing' your resume by filling it with quoted sections of the posted job description.

This tool can highlight them for you.

How does this work?
Step 1: Take the job description and paste the relevant portion into the Source / Job Description box.
Step 2: Put any expected or desired keywords into the Keywords box.
Step 3: Click 'Find Keywords'. The source text will be reproduced and each keyword found within it will be marked.






(If you have keywords you know will always be relevant, you can add them to the 'defaultKeywords' array in the <script> section.)
